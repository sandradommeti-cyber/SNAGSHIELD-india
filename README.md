# 🛵 SNAGSHIELD
### Small inconvenience. Smarter engineering.

**SNAGSHIELD** is a student-led product innovation project exploring a removable protective housing for scooter side stands.

## 💡 The Problem
Clothing can get caught on exposed scooter side-stand components while riders mount or dismount their vehicles. This can cause inconvenience, fabric damage and unexpected discomfort.

## 🚀 Our Proposed Concept
A removable, two-piece protective housing designed to cover the exposed side-stand rod and foot-plate edges while retaining the original stand mechanism.

### Design Objectives
- Smooth external profile
- Removable attachment
- Protection against clothing snagging
- Compatibility with existing side stands
- No replacement of the original stand mechanism

## 🔬 Development Roadmap
1. Problem identification
2. Existing product and patent research
3. Concept development
4. 3D modelling
5. Material and attachment selection
6. Prototype development
7. Testing and improvement

## 📍 Current Status
Concept development and preliminary research.

## 🤝 Collaboration
Open to learning, feedback and collaboration with mechanical engineers, industrial designers, CAD designers and 3D-printing enthusiasts.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer
SNAGSHIELD is an experimental concept. Its effectiveness, compatibility, durability and safety have not yet been validated. It is not an approved vehicle accessory.
